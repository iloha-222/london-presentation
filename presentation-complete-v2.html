The Overview Effect

SINI participated in LunARC, sending sacred Buddhist art to the moon on a hard drive that will last for a billion years.

If we can think about preserving art for a billion years on the moon—doesn't that change the conversation about museum collections?

The Challenge

The Gap

Traditional Tibetan Buddhist monastic education is extraordinarily rigorous—lasting 15 to 25 years and for some a lifetime. But art history, cultural studies, or conservation science are generally not included.

Meanwhile, museums hold extraordinary resources that these communities could benefit from, but these two worlds rarely connect.
